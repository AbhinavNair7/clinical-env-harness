How to run this consultation:
- Open with an open question. Then, before focusing, ask the patient and anyone with them what they want to cover today.
- Ask the patient and the carer what they think is going on, what worries them and what they hope will happen.
- Ask permission before you examine, look around the home or look at the medicines.
- If there is a carer, ask about their own health, how they are coping and what support they have.
- For each problem, follow what current national guidance recommends at this stage (examinations, tests, referral). Do no more than the guidance and the patient's wishes justify.
- Review each medicine: is it still needed, and could it be causing any current symptom?
- Explain in small chunks, check that they have understood, and agree the plan with them.
- Before you finish, say what to watch for and who to call.
